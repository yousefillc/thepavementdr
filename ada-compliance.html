California ADA & Title 24 Requirements

Protect Against ADA Lawsuits & Fines

California is one of the most active states for parking lot accessibility litigation. Non-compliant stall dimensions, missing blue curb markings, fading paint, or incorrect ramp slopes can expose property managers to substantial penalties. Proper re-striping ensures strict adherence to current statutes.

California Title 24 Specific Standards

In addition to federal regulations, California Title 24 mandates specific blue background box stenciling, white international wheelchair symbols, mandatory "NO PARKING" hatch lines in loading access aisles, and steel handicap signage posted at specific heights.
