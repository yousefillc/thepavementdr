Our Specialized Care

Full-Service Asphalt Maintenance

From single driveway restoration to multi-acre commercial parking lots, we prescribe the exact fix your asphalt needs.

Commercial Asphalt Sealcoating

Squeegee and spray application using heavy-duty mineral-filled emulsion. Oils and petrol spot priming included to protect against fluid leaks and severe weather.

  • Double-coat protection
  • Restores deep jet-black finish

Hot-Rubber Crack Filling

Direct-fire hot-pour rubber sealant injected at 375°F to fill open cracks. Prevents moisture intrusion into the subbase, stopping potholes before they form.

  • Flexible hot-pour rubber
  • Stops sub-base erosion

Line Striping & ADA Compliance

High-visibility layout restriping for parking stalls, red fire lanes, crosswalks, loading zones, and California Building Code (CBC) Title 24 handicap spaces.

  • Title 24 & ADA compliant
  • Commercial traffic paint

Pothole Patching & Repair

Saw-cut excavation, base compaction, and hot-mix asphalt patching to eliminate slip-and-fall hazards and vehicle damage liabilities.

  • Full depth asphalt repair
  • Compacted smooth finish
