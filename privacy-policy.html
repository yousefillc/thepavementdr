Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 23, 2026

At The Pavement Doctor, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or request our commercial asphalt, sealcoating, and striping services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when requesting a service quote, scheduling an inspection, or contacting our team. This may include:

  • Contact Information: First and last name, business email address, phone number, and physical mailing address.
  • Property Details: Commercial property address, pavement conditions, lot square footage, and project timeline needs.
  • Communication Data: Details submitted through quote forms, email inquiries, or phone call logs.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for legitimate commercial and business operations, including:

  • Preparing accurate job estimates, proposals, and scheduling site inspections.
  • Communicating regarding project status, weather delays, or work completion.
  • Processing invoices, payments, and service contracts.
  • Improving our website, customer support, and service offerings.

3. Information Sharing & Protection

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

We may share relevant job details only with trusted partners or subcontractors directly involved in executing your project (such as material suppliers or traffic control partners), or if required by law or legal process.

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational security measures to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse of your personal data.

4. Cookies & Analytics

Our website may use basic cookies and tracking technologies (such as Google Analytics) to analyze web traffic, evaluate site navigation performance, and enhance visitor experience. You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser settings at any time.
