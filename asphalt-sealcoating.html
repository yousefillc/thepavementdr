Why Commercial Asphalt Sealcoating Matters

Protection Against Southern California Sun

Unsealed asphalt absorbs intense ultraviolet (UV) radiation, leading to rapid binder oxidation. As the liquid asphalt binder breaks down, the pavement turns gray, becomes brittle, and loses flexibility, causing surface unraveling and premature cracking.

Resistance to Oils & Chemical Spills

Commercial parking lots experience constant vehicle drip from oil, gas, and automotive fluids. Sealcoat acts as an impenetrable barrier, preventing petroleum solvents from softening and dissolving the underlying asphalt foundation.

Our Professional Sealcoating Process

Phase Operational Step Benefit to Property Owners
Step 1 Surface Preparation & Power Sweeping Removes dirt, debris, grease, and loose gravel so sealcoat bonds directly to asphalt.
Step 2 Hot Rubber Crack Filling Seals open cracks (1/2" or larger) to eliminate water intrusion under the base.
Step 3 Commercial Emulsion Application Applies commercial-grade sealcoat with silica sand additives for slip resistance.
Step 4 Curing & Precision Re-Striping Restores rich jet-black finish and applies high-visibility line striping after 24-hour cure.
